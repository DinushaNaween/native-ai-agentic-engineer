## What Is an AI Agentic Engineer?

An AI Agentic Engineer builds:
- 🤖 **AI Agents** — autonomous systems that perceive, plan, and act
- 🐝 **Multi-Agent Swarms** — teams of specialized agents working together
- 📦 **RAG Systems** — LLMs connected to your own data
- 🚀 **Production AI** — scalable, observable, cost-efficient AI systems
